# LinkedIn post draft (day 2)

Native LinkedIn analytics told me a post "outperformed" because it hit 8,400 impressions.

I scored the same export against the people I actually want to work with: Heads of Content, Director+ , NL and EU, company size 201+.

Fourteen percent of that reach was inside the persona. Eighty-six percent was wasted reach. Students, junior recruiters, and a network still centred on Indian metros while I live and hire in the Netherlands.

The file never left the browser. No scrape, no API, no risk to the account.

I left out follower counts, scheduling, and any login. Those are ego metrics and distribution tools. This is a diagnostic.

If you run content for a team of fifty, the sentence you owe leadership is not "we got 20k views." It is "enterprise Heads of Content went from 8% to 27% of active reach."
